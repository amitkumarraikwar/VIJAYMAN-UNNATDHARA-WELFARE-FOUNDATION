# Missing information checklist

- [ ] Confirm CIN against official MCA records.
- [ ] Confirm official address and locality spelling.
- [ ] Confirm Section 8 licence number.
- [ ] Confirm incorporation date, ROC and company status.
- [ ] Provide an approved mission and vision.
- [ ] Provide approved director biographies and photographs.
- [ ] Provide genuine initiative, event, news and gallery content.
- [ ] Provide reports, financial statements and registration certificates for publication.
- [ ] Authorize phone number, office hours, map location and social profiles.
- [ ] Provide approved donation bank/payment details, receipt process, tax status and refund policy if donations will be enabled.
- [ ] Configure and test a real contact/volunteer submission backend, spam protection and privacy handling.
- [ ] Have legal pages reviewed by an authorized representative or legal adviser.
- [ ] Replace the placeholder site origin with the confirmed production domain.
